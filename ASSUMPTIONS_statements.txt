A1. Non-relativistic: Schrödinger equation, not Dirac
A2. Hamiltonian is time-independent
A3. Any state is a superposition of stationary states (complete set)
A4. Box with infinitely hard walls (ψ = 0 at the boundary)
A5. Rectangular box: 2D/3D wave is a product of 1D waves
A6. Potential depends only on the distance r from the nucleus
A7. Point nucleus of infinite mass
A8. Hydrogen levels from the Coulomb attraction only (no fine structure, Lamb shift or hyperfine)
A9. Born-Oppenheimer: nuclei fixed, electrons solved at each geometry
A10. Label energy is one geometry, ground state, no vibrations, no temperature
A11. Orbital approximation: one orbital per electron
A12. Wavefunction antisymmetric under electron exchange (Pauli)
A13. LCAO: orbitals are combinations of atom-centred functions
A14. Only N fixed basis functions instead of a complete set
A15. Basis functions are non-orthogonal (overlap matrix S)
A16. Gaussians replace Slater functions (contracted, e.g. STO-nG)
A17. Shells with fixed angular parts; add zeta, polarization (P) and diffuse (D) functions
A18. Hartree-Fock: one Slater determinant, mean field
A19. Self-consistent field (SCF) loop solved to a threshold
A20. Kohn-Sham: the energy is a functional of the density
A21. Finite basis set (def2-TZVPD for OMol25, def2-TZVPPD for SPICE II)
A22. Exchange-correlation approximated (ωB97M-V: meta-GGA range-separated hybrid)
A23. Range separation: ω = 0.3 bohr⁻¹, 15% short-range and 100% long-range exact exchange
A24. Dispersion added separately: VV10 (OMol25) or D3(BJ) (SPICE II)
A25. Finite integration grid (OMol25: DEFGRID3)
A26. RI-J and COSX speed-ups (OMol25, ORCA 6.0.0)
A27. Unrestricted Kohn-Sham for open-shell and charged systems
A28. Protein-ligand interaction energy = E(ligand+pocket) − E(ligand) − E(pocket)
A29. The DFT label is treated as truth when training ML potentials or force fields
A30. Effective core potential replaces the core electrons of heavy atoms
A31. Different levels of theory for different jobs (OPLS5)
A32. ωB97X, ωB97X-D and ωB97X-D3 are different refitted functionals
A33. CBS: extrapolate to the complete basis set limit
A34. Classical force field with fitted parameters replaces the quantum calculation
A35. Single reference: one determinant dominates the wavefunction
A36. CCSD truncated at doubles, triples added perturbatively (T)
